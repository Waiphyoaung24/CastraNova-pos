## 1. Positioning

NorthByte gives clients **direction**, not just deliverables. The name carries the strategy:
**North** (guidance, true north, judgment) + **Byte** (digital craft, precision). The studio
that points clients the right way and executes to a high finish.

- **One-liner:** *NorthByte — creative direction, precisely built.*
- **Positioning statement:** For founders and organizations who need design that earns trust,
  NorthByte is the studio that pairs editorial craft with strategic direction — premium work,
  without the agency bloat.
- **Three pillars:**
  - **Direction** — we decide what matters and make the call.
  - **Craft** — high-finish execution; the details are the product.
  - **Range** — flexes from startup launch to enterprise rollout without losing the premium feel.

---

## 2. Verbal Identity

- **Personality:** refined, assured, economical. The expert in the room who doesn't oversell.
- **Voice principles:**
  - Confident and plain. Short sentences.
  - No hype words, no exclamation points, no jargon.
  - Say the true thing simply; let the work carry the weight.
- **Do / Don't:**
  - ✅ "We make the call and stand behind it."
  - ❌ "We're a passionate, synergistic team of rockstars!"
- **Messaging pillars (for copy):** Direction · Craft · Range (mirror the positioning pillars).

## 3. Visual Identity — Editorial Ink

### Color tokens

| Token | Hex | Role / usage rule |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#16161A` | Primary. Text, dark surfaces, the monogram on paper. |
| Paper | `#F4F2ED` | Background. Warm off-white — the default canvas. |
| Gold | `#9B8C70` | Accent **only**. Sparing emphasis — rules, the "Byte" italic, small marks. Never a fill for large areas. |

Rule of thumb: **gold is seasoning, never the meal.** A page should read as ink-on-paper first.

### Typography

- **Display / headlines:** an elegant serif (editorial feel) — to be specified in the brand book
  (e.g. a high-contrast transitional/Didone-adjacent serif).
- **Body / UI:** a clean neutral sans for readability and interface use.
- **Wordmark detail:** "Byte" set in *italic* to mark the North→Byte pivot.

### Logo system

- **Primary mark:** **NB monogram** — contained lockup that stands alone (favicon, avatar, stamp,
  merch). Works on paper and on ink/dark backgrounds.
- **Full lockup:** **NorthByte** wordmark (serif, italic "Byte") for headers, signatures, formal use.
- **To be specified in the brand book:** clear-space, minimum sizes, monogram-vs-wordmark usage
  matrix, and explicit don'ts (no stretching, no recoloring outside tokens, no added effects).

### Layout principles

- Generous whitespace; restraint over decoration.
- Editorial hierarchy — large serif headlines, quiet body, the work as the hero.

## 4. Deliverable: the Brand Book

A single Markdown document (committed to the repo) with these sections:

1. **Brand story & positioning** — name rationale, one-liner, positioning statement, pillars.
2. **Voice & messaging** — personality, voice principles, do/don't examples, messaging pillars.
3. **Color** — tokens (hex), usage rules, the gold-as-seasoning rule.
4. **Typography** — display + body choices, scale, the italic-"Byte" detail.
5. **Logo** — monogram + wordmark, usage matrix, clear-space, minimum sizes, don'ts.
6. **Application examples** — at least: business card / signature, social avatar, a simple web header,
   a document/letterhead treatment.

**Success criteria:** a designer or developer unfamiliar with this conversation could open the brand
book and produce on-brand work without further input.

---

## 5. Out of scope (YAGNI)

- No full website build, marketing campaign, or pitch deck (brand book only).
- No multi-sub-brand / product naming system — single master brand for now.
- No motion/animation guidelines unless requested later.
