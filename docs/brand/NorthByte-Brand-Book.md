## 1 · Brand Foundation

### What we do
NorthByte is a premium creative and design studio. We give clients **direction** — not just deliverables. We decide what matters, then execute it to a high finish: brand, digital, and product design that earns trust.

### Who we serve
A deliberately mixed roster, unified by a standard rather than a size:
- **Founders & early-stage teams** who need a credible brand and product to launch.
- **Established organizations & enterprise** who need a refresh or rollout that still feels considered.

The through-line: clients who value judgment and finish over volume and noise.

### Where we're going
The name is built to scale. **North** keeps us anchored to guidance and judgment even as services expand; **Byte** keeps us credible in digital craft. NorthByte can grow from a boutique studio into a full creative practice — strategy, brand systems, product — without renaming. It reads as seriously at a Fortune 500 procurement table as it does in a founder's DM.

## 2 · Naming

### The name: NorthByte

A **compound** name joining two ideas that hold the brand's tension:

- **North** — direction, guidance, *true north*, judgment. The strategic half.
- **Byte** — digital craft, precision, the made thing. The execution half.

Together they say what the studio is: *we point the right way, and we build it precisely.*

### Why it works
- **Future-proof.** Names the value (direction + craft), not a single service, so it survives expansion.
- **Enterprise-safe.** Dignified and pronounceable; no gimmick to outgrow (the DemoChimp trap).

## 3 · Positioning

### Positioning statement

> For **founders and organizations who need design that earns trust**,
> **NorthByte** is a **premium creative & design studio**
> that **pairs strategic direction with high-finish execution**.
> Unlike **volume agencies and freelance marketplaces**,
> we **make the call and build it precisely — judgment and craft, not just hours.**

### Three pillars

1. **Direction** — We decide what matters and stand behind the call. Strategy before pixels.
2. **Craft** — High-finish execution. The details *are* the product.
3. **Range** — Flexes from startup launch to enterprise rollout without losing the premium feel.

### Key differentiators
1. **Judgment included.** Clients get a point of view, not just production.
2. **Editorial finish.** Restraint and craft as the default, not an upsell.
3. **No bloat.** Premium outcomes without the layers of a traditional agency.


NorthByte owns the **high-craft + right-sized-touch** quadrant: agency-grade finish, without agency overhead.

## 4 · Brand Voice

### Personality traits

1. **Assured** — We state, we don't hedge. "We make the call and stand behind it."
2. **Economical** — Few words, well chosen. Short sentences. No filler.
3. **Refined** — Plain but elevated. The expert in the room who doesn't oversell.

### Voice spectrum

| Dimension | Our position |
|---|---|
| Formal ↔ Casual | `○○●○○` — centered, leaning formal |
| Serious ↔ Playful | `○●○○○` — serious, with restraint |
| Expert ↔ Approachable | `○○●○○` — expert, but not cold |
| Reserved ↔ Bold | `○○●○○` — quietly confident |

### We are / We are not

| We ARE | We are NOT |
|---|---|
| Assured | Arrogant |
| Plain-spoken | Jargon-heavy |
| Restrained | Loud |
| Considered | Trend-chasing |
| Warm-precise | Corporate-cold |

### Writing rules
- **Do:** short sentences; say the true thing simply; let the work carry the weight.
- **Don't:** hype words ("rockstar," "synergy," "passionate," "game-changing"), exclamation points, jargon, or filler adjectives.

### Voice examples

**How we say hello:**
> "NorthByte. We give brands direction — and build it precisely."

**How we explain the studio:**
> "We're a design studio. We figure out what matters, then make it to a high finish. Strategy and craft, in one place."

**How we describe a project:**
> "We rebuilt their identity from the positioning up. Quieter, sharper, easier to trust."

**How we handle a decline / boundary:**
> "This isn't the right fit for how we work — but here's who we'd point you to."

## 5 · Visual Identity — Editorial Ink

The system reads as **ink on paper first.** Whitespace and restraint do the work; gold is the only flourish, used sparingly.

### Color tokens

| Token | Hex | RGB | Role & rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ink** | `#16161A` | 22, 22, 26 | Primary. Body text, dark surfaces, the monogram on paper. |
| **Paper** | `#F4F2ED` | 244, 242, 237 | Background. Warm off-white — the default canvas. |
| **Gold** | `#9B8C70` | 155, 140, 112 | Accent **only**. Hairline rules, the italic "Byte," small marks, single points of emphasis. **Never** a large fill. |

**Suggested neutrals (for UI/body comfort):**

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Ink-70 | `#4A4A50` | Secondary text, captions |
| Line | `#DEDAD0` | Hairline dividers on paper |

**The rule of thumb:** *gold is seasoning, never the meal.* If a layout reads as "gold," there's too much. A page should read ink-on-paper, with gold noticed only on a second look.

**Contrast note:** Ink on Paper is high-contrast and accessible for body text. Gold on Paper is for **decorative/large or non-essential** use only — do **not** set body copy in gold on paper (it falls below WCAG AA for small text). For text emphasis, prefer Ink; reserve gold for the wordmark detail, rules, and marks.

### Typography

| Role | Direction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Display / headlines** | Elegant high-contrast serif | Editorial feel — a transitional/Didone-adjacent serif. Suggested: *Canela, GT Sectra, Newsreader*, or system fallback `Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif`. |
| **Body / UI** | Clean neutral sans | Readable, quiet. Suggested: *Inter, Söhne, Neue Haas Grotesk*, or fallback `-apple-system, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`. |

**Type principles:**
- Large serif headlines, generous leading, quiet sans body.
- One display serif + one body sans — no third family.
- Numerals and small caps welcome for an editorial touch.

**Suggested scale (px / web):**

| Level | Size / Line | Family |
|---|---|---|
| Display | 48–64 / 1.1 | Serif |
| H1 | 34 / 1.2 | Serif |
| H2 | 24 / 1.3 | Serif |
| Body | 16–18 / 1.6 | Sans |
| Caption | 13 / 1.4 | Sans (Ink-70) |

### Logo system

**Primary mark — NB monogram.** A contained "NB" lockup that stands alone. The "N" in Ink, the "B" in Gold, set in the display serif inside a rounded container with a hairline gold rule. Works on Paper and reversed on Ink.

```
   ┌─────────┐
   │  N B    │      N = Ink   B = Gold
   │         │      Container: hairline gold rule, ~6px radius
   └─────────┘      Reverses cleanly onto Ink (#16161A)
```

**Full lockup — wordmark.** **North** in the display serif (Ink), **Byte** in *italic* serif. Use for headers, signatures, formal documents, and the website masthead.

```
   North𝘉𝘺𝘵𝘦        ← "Byte" set in italic; optional gold italic for accent
```

**Usage matrix:**

| Context | Mark |
|---|---|
| Favicon, avatar, app icon, stamp, merch tag | **NB monogram** |
| Website header, email signature, letterhead, decks | **Full wordmark** |
| Dark backgrounds | Either, reversed (Paper on Ink; monogram "B" stays Gold) |
| Very small (<24px) | **Monogram** only |

**Clear space:** keep a margin equal to the height of the "N" (wordmark) or the container's corner radius ×4 (monogram) clear of other elements on all sides.

**Minimum sizes:** monogram ≥ 16px tall; wordmark ≥ 100px wide.

**Logo don'ts:**
- ✗ Don't recolor outside the three tokens (no full-gold logo, no off-brand colors).
- ✗ Don't stretch, skew, rotate, or add drop shadows / glows / gradients.
- ✗ Don't set "Byte" upright in the wordmark (the italic is the brand detail).
- ✗ Don't place on busy photography without an Ink or Paper scrim.
- ✗ Don't reconstruct the monogram in a different typeface.

### Layout principles
- **Whitespace is the brand.** Generous margins; let elements breathe.
- **Restraint over decoration.** One accent, one idea per view.
- **Editorial hierarchy.** Big serif headline, quiet body, the work as hero.
- **Hairlines, not boxes.** Gold/Line hairline rules to divide, rather than heavy borders or fills.

## 6 · Application Examples

**Business card**
> Paper stock. Front: NB monogram centered, small. Back: name + role in sans (Ink), a single gold hairline rule, contact in Ink-70. No clutter.

**Email signature**
> `NorthByte` wordmark (italic "Byte"), then name · role in sans, then one gold hairline, then contact line in Ink-70.

**Web header**
> Paper background. Left: wordmark. Right: 3–4 sans nav links in Ink. A single gold hairline under the header. Hero: oversized serif headline, short sans subhead.

**Letterhead / document**
> Monogram top-left, gold hairline rule across the top margin, serif title, sans body. Footer in Ink-70.

**Social avatar**
> NB monogram on Ink (reversed), "B" in gold. Reads at 32px.
